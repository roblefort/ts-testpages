Two paths to the same paperwork

Why it matters who handles your application.

A Permanent Resident Visa is not a form to file. It is a legal status that anchors your tax position when you eventually sell. The party who prepares it should be qualified to defend it.

An unqualified intermediary

The "tramitador" route.

Common, cheap, and undocumented. A facilitator submits paperwork on your behalf with no legal standing.

  • No legal accountability if your application is rejected or delayed.
  • No advisory on how your residency status interacts with future capital gains tax.
  • No coordination with the notary who will eventually calculate your sale tax.
  • No file, no record, no continuity if anything is challenged years later.
  • You are alone the day a tax discrepancy surfaces at closing.
A real estate law firm

The Title Solutions route.

Your immigration file becomes part of your real estate file — handled by the same attorneys who will close your sale.

  • Bilingual senior attorneys with legal accountability for the work.
  • Strategic advisory: your residency is structured around your eventual sale.
  • Direct coordination with the notary, the trustee bank, and the SAT.
  • A complete, retrievable legal file you and your heirs can rely on.
  • One firm from acquisition, through residency, to closing.
Why Consider a Permanent Resident Visa

Three benefits, one decision.

01

Material Tax Advantages on Sale

A Permanent Resident Visa allows foreign sellers to claim the property as their primary residence, unlocking deductions and exemptions under Mexican law that can meaningfully reduce capital gains tax due at closing.

02

Long‑Term Security & Peace of Mind

Legal certainty for you and your family. Simpler banking, healthcare access, and day‑to‑day matters in Mexico. A stronger overall investment position from the moment your card is issued.

03

Future‑Proofing Your Investment

By obtaining resident status now — often years before you actually decide to sell — you position yourself to maximize the net proceeds when the moment comes. It is preparation, not paperwork.
